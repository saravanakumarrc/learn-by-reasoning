# Phase 22: Responsible AI & Governance

**Stage:** AI Solution Architect

## Why this phase exists here

Comes late on purpose — governance questions (bias, explainability, regulation) are easiest to reason about once you've actually built RAG, agents, and multi-agent systems and can point to concrete decisions that need governing.

## The question this phase should leave you able to answer

> Why does 'the model said so' not count as an explanation for a loan denial?

## Sections in this phase
- **[Learn](01-learn/README.md)** — Governance, privacy, explainability, and the regulatory landscape (EU AI Act, GDPR) an enterprise architect has to navigate.
