# Phase 8: Technical Leadership & Delivery

**Stage:** Technical Lead

## Why this phase exists here

Your product vision names Technical Lead as a distinct stage. This phase is what actually differentiates a Tech Lead from a strong Senior Developer — delivery and people skills, not more technology.

## The question this phase should leave you able to answer

> Why is a two-line PR comment sometimes more valuable than rewriting the code yourself?

## Sections in this phase
- **[Code & delivery practices](01-code-delivery-practices/README.md)** — How you multiply your own judgment through a team, instead of being the sole bottleneck.
- **[Incident & operational leadership](02-incident-operational-leadership/README.md)** — Owning outcomes during a fire, not just writing code before one.
- **[People & process](03-people-process/README.md)** — The non-technical skills — mentoring, hiring, conflict resolution — that pure technical study never teaches.
