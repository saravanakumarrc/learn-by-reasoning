# Phase 20: AI Cost Architecture

## Why this phase exists here

Extends Phase 7's general cost/FinOps reasoning into AI-specific economics, where the numbers behave very differently from ordinary compute costs.

> Why might caching save you more money than switching to a cheaper model?
